ACME CORP BENEFITS SUMMARY
===========================

This plain-text summary gives an overview of the benefits offered to full-time
employees of Acme Corp. Enrolment happens during onboarding and during the
annual open enrolment window each November.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Acme Corp offers a default Preferred Provider Organisation (PPO) health plan
covering employees and their dependents. The company pays 80 percent of the
employee premium and 60 percent of dependent premiums. Dental and vision
coverage are included at no additional cost. The annual deductible is 500 USD
for in-network care.

RETIREMENT

Employees are auto-enrolled in the 401(k) retirement plan at a 5 percent
contribution rate. Acme Corp matches 100 percent of the first 5 percent of
contributions, effectively doubling that portion of savings. Vesting is
immediate.

LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE

Group life insurance of two times annual salary and long-term disability
coverage at 60 percent of salary are provided at no cost. Supplemental coverage
can be purchased during open enrolment.

WELLNESS

The wellness benefit reimburses 300 USD per calendar year for gym memberships,
fitness equipment, or wellness programs. The Employee Assistance Program
provides 8 confidential counselling sessions per issue per year.

LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,000 USD for courses,
conferences, and books, subject to manager approval. Degree programmes have a
separate tuition assistance cap of 5,000 USD per year after one full year of
service.
