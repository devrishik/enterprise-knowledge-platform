# IT Policy

## Equipment

Every full-time employee receives a standard laptop (14-inch, 16 GB RAM)
refreshed every 3 years. Upgrades require manager approval and a documented
business need. Monitors, docks, and keyboards can be ordered through the IT
portal with manager approval.

## Software Requests

Only software from the approved catalog may be installed on corporate devices.
Requests for new software are submitted through the IT portal and are reviewed
for security and licensing compliance within 10 working days. Installing
unapproved software violates the Security Policy.

## Accounts and Access

Access to systems is granted through role-based groups. Managers must review
their team's access quarterly. Access is revoked within 24 hours of an
employee's departure, and immediately upon termination for cause.

## VPN

The corporate VPN is required for access to internal systems when working
remotely. Split tunneling is disabled. VPN issues should be reported through
the IT helpdesk; do not use personal VPN services on corporate devices.

## Backups

Employees must store all work product in approved locations (corporate drive,
approved cloud storage). Local-only files are not backed up and are not
protected against device failure.

## Printing

Confidential documents may only be printed on printers in secured areas and
must be collected immediately. Personal printers may not be connected to
corporate devices.

## Support

The IT helpdesk operates 08:00–18:00 local time on working days. Critical
production incidents follow the escalation path in the Security Policy rather
than the normal helpdesk queue.
