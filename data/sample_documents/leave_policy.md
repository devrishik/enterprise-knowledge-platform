# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to 22 days of paid annual leave per leave
year, accruing at approximately 1.83 days per month of service. This Leave
Policy is the authoritative source for leave entitlements and supersedes any
summary in other documents.

## Carryover

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next
leave year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March of the following
year, after which they expire.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave,
which may be taken in blocks of no less than one week within 12 months of the
child's arrival.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is
required for any absence longer than three consecutive working days. Sick
leave does not carry over between years.

## Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted for personal, educational, or
humanitarian reasons with approval from both the line manager and People
Operations. Requests must be submitted at least 30 days in advance.

## Requesting Leave

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working
days before the intended start date for periods up to one week, and 20 working
days in advance for longer periods. Managers respond within 5 working days.
