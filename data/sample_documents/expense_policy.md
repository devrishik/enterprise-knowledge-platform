# Expense Policy

## General Principles

Expenses must be actual, reasonable, business-related, and supported by
itemised receipts. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after they were
incurred require CFO approval.

## Meal Per Diem

The daily meal per diem is 60 USD for domestic travel and 75 USD for
international travel. No receipts are required for per diem claims. Team meals
during travel follow the per diem of the most senior attendee's destination.

## Accommodation

Hotel reimbursement is capped at 180 USD per night in domestic cities and 240
USD per night in international cities. Higher amounts require pre-approval
from Finance at least 3 working days before booking.

## Flights and Rail

Domestic flights must be economy class. Business class is permitted for single
legs longer than 6 hours with VP approval. Rail travel is encouraged for
journeys under 4 hours where available.

## Approval Thresholds

Expenses above 500 USD in total require manager pre-approval; expenses above
2,000 USD require director pre-approval. All approvals must be recorded in the
expense system before the cost is incurred, not after.

## Client Entertainment

Client entertainment is limited to 150 USD per person per event and requires a
written business purpose. Alcohol may not be expensed for internal team events
without HR approval.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Traffic fines, airline lounge memberships, personal seat upgrades, hotel mini-
bar charges, and expenses lacking itemised receipts are not reimbursable.
