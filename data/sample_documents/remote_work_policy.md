# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines who may work remotely, how often, and the support Acme
Corp provides for home offices. It exists to balance flexibility with
collaboration and information security.

## Eligibility

Remote work is available to all permanent employees after completing their
probation period of 90 days. Roles that require physical presence, such as
facilities and hardware lab staff, are excluded.

## Office Attendance Requirement

Hybrid employees must be present in the office at least three days per week,
including one fixed team anchor day set by each department. Fully remote
arrangements require VP-level approval and are reviewed every six months.

## Home Office Stipend

Employees working remotely at least one day per week receive a one-time home
office stipend of 500 USD, usable for desks, chairs, monitors, and lighting.
Internet costs are reimbursed up to 40 USD per month upon submission of
receipts through the expense system.

## Security Requirements

Remote workers must connect through the corporate VPN for all work involving
internal systems. Personal printers may not be used for company documents.
Devices must remain locked when unattended, and screens must not be visible
from public spaces. See the Security Policy for incident reporting duties.

## Tax and Legal Residence

Employees may not work remotely from a different country for more than 30 days
per calendar year without written approval from Legal and Payroll, due to
permanent-establishment and tax residency rules.

## Meetings and Availability

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core collaboration hours
(10:00–16:00 local time), keep their calendar current, and join meetings with
video on by default for onboarding and client meetings.
