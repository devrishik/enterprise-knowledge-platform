# Employee Handbook — Acme Corp

## Welcome

Welcome to Acme Corp. This handbook summarises the core policies that apply to
all full-time employees. Where this handbook conflicts with a dedicated policy
document, the dedicated policy document is considered authoritative, and the
discrepancy should be reported to People Operations.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to act with integrity, respect, and professionalism.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are strictly prohibited and are
grounds for disciplinary action up to and including termination. Gifts from
vendors above 50 USD must be declined or reported to Compliance.

## Paid Time Off

Employees receive 18 days of paid time off per calendar year, accruing at
1.5 days per month. Unused days may be carried over, subject to the detailed
rules in the Leave Policy document. Public holidays are published annually and
do not count against paid time off.

## Remote Work Basics

Acme Corp supports flexible working. Employees may work remotely up to two
days per week with manager approval, provided they maintain core collaboration
hours between 10:00 and 16:00 local time. Detailed eligibility rules, home
office support, and security requirements are defined in the Remote Work
Policy.

## Working Hours and Overtime

Standard working hours are 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday. Overtime must
be approved in advance by a department head. Non-exempt employees are paid
overtime at 1.5x their hourly rate.

## Anti-Bribery and Compliance

Acme Corp complies with all applicable anti-bribery laws. No employee may
offer, give, or authorize any payment or gift intended to improperly influence
a business decision. Suspected violations must be reported through the
anonymous ethics hotline.

## Leaving Acme

Employees resigning from Acme Corp are asked to provide at least 30 days
written notice. Company equipment must be returned during the exit interview,
and access to systems is revoked on the last working day.
