# Security Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who
access Acme Corp information systems, whether on premises or remote.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must not reuse any of the
previous 10 passwords. Password rotation is required every 90 days for
accounts with access to production systems and every 365 days for standard
accounts. Password managers approved by IT are the recommended storage.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all corporate accounts. Hardware
security keys are issued to engineers with production access. SMS-based
factors are not accepted for privileged accounts.

## Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents, lost devices, or phishing messages must be
reported to security@acme.test within 24 hours of discovery. Do not attempt to
investigate or contain incidents yourself unless you are part of the Security
Operations team.

## Data Classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Confidential data may not be stored on personal devices or non-corporate
cloud services. Restricted data requires approval from the Data Protection
Officer before any processing outside the approved systems list.

## Device Security

Corporate laptops enforce full-disk encryption, automatic screen lock after 10
minutes of inactivity, and centralized endpoint management. Installing
unapproved software on corporate devices is prohibited; see the IT Policy for
the software request process.

## Third-Party Access

Vendors requiring system access must sign the standard Data Processing
Addendum, use named accounts with multi-factor authentication, and undergo
quarterly access reviews by the Security team.
