Travel Policy

Booking Channels

All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Bookings made outside the portal are not reimbursable unless the portal was unavailable and the trip was pre-approved by Finance.

Class of Service

Flights under 6 hours must be economy class. For single legs of 6 hours or longer, premium economy is permitted. Business class requires VP approval regardless of flight length.

Advance Booking

Domestic trips should be booked at least 14 days in advance and international trips at least 21 days in advance to secure corporate fares. Changes made within 72 hours of departure require a reason code in the expense system.

Travel Insurance

Travel medical insurance is included in the corporate travel portal rates. Additional travel insurance should not be purchased separately and will not be reimbursed.

Ground Transport

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursable for airport transfers when public transport is impractical. Car rental is limited to compact class unless three or more employees are travelling together.

Visas and Documentation

Visa fees and required vaccinations are reimbursable with receipts. Passport fees are personal and not reimbursable. The travel desk assists with visa applications; submit requests at least 6 weeks before departure. Meal per diems and hotel caps follow the Expense Policy.
